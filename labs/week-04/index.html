The exercise

A practice exercise based on your brief.

Minimum

  1. Create a scene, perspective camera and WebGLRenderer.
  2. Make a faceted crystal with a material that responds to light.
  3. Add AmbientLight and DirectionalLight. Compare how each illuminates the crystal.
  4. Add OrbitControls to inspect the crystal from every side.

Standard

  1. Add controls for each light's visibility, color and intensity.
  2. Move the directional light along X, Y and Z. Observe the changing highlights.
  3. Control crystal color and roughness. Compare polished and matte surfaces.

Challenge

  1. Enable directional shadows on a floor and pedestal.
  2. Add a DirectionalLightHelper and an optional wireframe.
  3. Animate the crystal using elapsed time; add pause, speed and reset controls.

Try it

Turn off the directional light: the facets lose contrast. Turn off both lights: the crystal becomes dark. Restore the directional light and change roughness from 0.05 to 1 to compare highlights.
